Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 16, 2026

MediaKey Solutions Inc. ("Staia," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Staia mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we share it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

Staia is an AI-powered astrology app. Because astrology chat can feel personal, please read this policy carefully before sharing information in the App. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, do not use the App.

1. Scope and Controller

This Privacy Policy applies to the Staia mobile application, our public website pages that link to this policy, and support communications with us. MediaKey Solutions Inc. is the controller of the personal information we collect and decide how to process.

This policy does not replace the privacy policies of third-party services that you choose to use with Staia, such as Apple, Google, or payment platforms. Those services process some information under their own terms and privacy policies.

2. Our Privacy Commitments

  • We do not sell your chat messages, birth chart data, or account information.
  • We do not use your chat messages or birth chart data for advertising.
  • Your Staia chats are not public by default.
  • We use your chat messages and birth chart context to provide, personalize, secure, and improve the App.
  • We share information only as described in this policy, including with service providers that help us run Staia.
  • We do not use your chat messages or birth chart data to train a Staia-owned foundation model.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: Email address, encrypted password, name, profile details, avatar images, public avatar URLs, and account identifiers.
  • Authentication information: Information we receive when you sign in with Apple, Google, or another supported authentication provider, such as your name, email address, provider identifier, and authentication tokens.
  • Birth chart information: Birth date, birth time, birth location, city, coordinates, timezone, name, gender, and related details used to calculate or personalize astrological content.
  • Chat messages and AI content: Messages you send, AI responses you receive, conversation history, conversation context, memories or summaries derived from conversations, saved preferences, and feedback you provide about responses.
  • Onboarding and preference information: Interests, goals, topics, notification preferences, and other choices you make in the App.
  • Support communications: Information you include when you contact us, including your email address, request details, attachments, and any follow-up messages.
  • Subscription information: Subscription status, purchase history, trial status, product identifiers, store transaction identifiers, and entitlement information. Apple or Google process your payment card details; we do not store full payment card numbers.

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and technical information: Device type, operating system, app version, device identifiers, push notification tokens, language, timezone, and general device settings.
  • Log and diagnostic information: Error logs, crash reports, performance data, timestamps, request metadata, and debugging information.
  • Usage and analytics information: Screens viewed, buttons tapped, features used, session activity, onboarding status, subscription status, feature flag exposure, and other event data that helps us understand and improve the App.

3.3 Sensitive Information

Staia is not designed for medical, mental health, legal, financial, or crisis support. Do not share information that you would only share with a licensed professional, including detailed trauma, self-harm intent, medical conditions, government identifiers, financial account details, passwords, or information about another person without their permission.

If you choose to include sensitive information in chat messages or support requests, we process it only for the purposes described in this policy, such as generating responses, preserving conversation continuity, providing support, maintaining safety, complying with law, and protecting the App.

4. How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain the App: Create accounts, authenticate users, calculate birth charts, provide AI chat, save conversation history, sync data, and deliver core features.
  • Personalize your experience: Use birth chart data, preferences, onboarding answers, conversation context, and saved history to tailor astrological content and AI responses.
  • Operate AI chat: Send the information needed for a response, such as your prompt, relevant conversation context, birth chart context, memory context, tool results, and technical metadata, to AI service providers that generate or support responses for Staia.
  • Manage subscriptions: Confirm purchases, manage trials, maintain entitlement status, prevent subscription abuse, and provide access to paid features.
  • Communicate with you: Respond to support requests, send administrative messages, notify you about important account or policy changes, and deliver push notifications where enabled.
  • Analyze and improve Staia: Understand feature usage, identify bugs, test changes, improve reliability, evaluate response quality, and develop new features.
  • Protect safety and security: Detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, security incidents, technical problems, policy violations, and activity that may harm users, us, or others.
  • Comply with law: Meet legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, and App Store or platform requirements, and enforce our Terms of Service.

5. AI Chat and Model Providers

Staia uses OpenRouter as an AI model gateway for AI-powered astrological responses and related AI tasks. OpenRouter may route requests to underlying model providers based on our configured model and provider settings. To provide this feature, we may send the AI service your prompt, relevant conversation context, birth chart context, memory context, tool results, and related technical metadata.

AI providers may process this information to generate responses, run safety checks, prevent abuse, maintain service reliability, debug issues, and comply with their own legal obligations. OpenRouter states that it does not store prompts and responses unless customers opt in to input/output logging, but underlying model providers have their own logging, retention, and training policies. We do not use your chat messages or birth chart data to train a Staia-owned foundation model.

We may store your chat history, conversation state, and conversation-derived memories in our systems so the App can provide conversation continuity and personalized responses. Account deletion and privacy requests are handled as described in the retention and rights sections below.

We use service providers to operate, secure, analyze, and improve Staia. These providers may process personal information only as needed to provide services to us and as described in their own policies and agreements.

  • Supabase: Database hosting, authentication, and related infrastructure. Privacy Policy
  • Railway: Cloud infrastructure used for app services and conversation-related systems. Privacy Policy
  • OpenRouter: AI model gateway, model-provider routing, AI response generation, and related safety, abuse-prevention, and service operations. Privacy Policy

  • Qdrant: Vector database infrastructure used for conversation memory storage and retrieval. Privacy Policy

  • Sign in with Apple: Apple ID authentication. Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Push notification delivery. Privacy Policy
  • Sentry: Error monitoring and crash reporting. Privacy Policy
  • Statsig: Analytics event tracking, experimentation, and feature flags. Privacy Policy
  • Airbridge: Mobile attribution, referral, and deep-link measurement. Privacy Policy
  • LangSmith: AI workflow tracing, debugging, and observability when enabled. Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare R2: Object storage used for profile avatar and media uploads. Privacy Policy

7. When We Share Information

We may share personal information in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: With vendors that host, process, analyze, secure, or support the App on our behalf.
  • App stores and payment platforms: With Apple, Google, RevenueCat, and similar providers to manage purchases, subscriptions, refunds, fraud prevention, and entitlement status.
  • Legal and safety reasons: When we believe disclosure is required by law, legal process, regulation, subpoena, court order, or government request, or is necessary to protect rights, privacy, safety, security, property, users, us, or others.
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, bankruptcy, sale of assets, or similar transaction involving all or part of our business.
  • With your direction or consent: When you ask us to disclose information or otherwise consent to the disclosure.
  • Aggregated or de-identified information: We may use or share information that has been aggregated or de-identified so it is no longer reasonably linked to you.

We do not sell personal information as the term "sell" is commonly understood. We also do not disclose chat messages or birth chart data to advertising partners for targeted advertising.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law. Retention depends on the type of information, your settings, whether your account is active, legal obligations, security needs, and whether deletion is technically possible in backups.

  • Account deletion: When you delete your account in the App, Staia deactivates the account, deletes the Supabase authentication user, and marks the related app user record as deleted. Signing in again creates a new account. We may preserve product history, conversation history, memory, usage, subscription, and audit records internally where needed for service integrity, legal obligations, safety, fraud prevention, accounting, analytics, and debugging. Direct user access to tombstoned account data is blocked.
  • Account, profile, birth chart, preferences, conversation history, and memory records: Retained while your account is active so we can provide the App, then deleted, de-identified, access-restricted, or retained after account deletion depending on service integrity, legal, security, backup, dispute, fraud-prevention, accounting, analytics, debugging, or platform-compliance needs.
  • Subscription and transaction records: Retained as needed for accounting, tax, audit, fraud-prevention, App Store, Google Play, and legal requirements.
  • Support communications: Retained as needed to respond to requests, maintain records of how we handled them, and protect legal rights.
  • Analytics, logs, and diagnostic data: Retained for a limited period appropriate to debugging, security, reliability, analytics, and product improvement, then deleted, aggregated, or de-identified where practical.
  • Backups: Deleted information may remain in encrypted backups for a limited period until those backups are overwritten or deleted in the ordinary course.

9. Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information, including HTTPS/TLS for data in transit, authentication controls, access restrictions, secure credential handling, monitoring, and provider security controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure and for using a secure device. Contact us promptly at support@staia.io if you believe your account or information has been compromised.

10. Your Choices and Rights

10.1 App Controls

  • You can update certain profile, birth chart, preference, and notification settings in the App.
  • You can manage push notification permissions in your device settings and, where available, in the App.
  • You can cancel subscriptions through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store subscription settings.
  • You can delete your account through the App where that feature is available, or by contacting us.

10.2 Privacy Requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, or information about how we process your personal information. You may also have the right to appeal a decision or lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

To exercise privacy rights, contact us at support@staia.io. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Some information may be exempt from deletion or access requests where law allows, such as information needed for security, fraud prevention, legal claims, accounting, or compliance.

11. U.S. State Privacy Rights

If you are a resident of California or another U.S. state with a comprehensive privacy law, you may have additional rights, including the right to know what categories of personal information we collect, use, and disclose; the right to access or delete certain information; the right to correct inaccurate information; the right to opt out of certain sales, sharing, or targeted advertising; and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising privacy rights.

We do not sell personal information or share chat messages or birth chart data for targeted advertising. To submit a U.S. state privacy request, contact us at support@staia.io.

12. European, UK, and Swiss Privacy Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, our legal bases for processing may include performance of a contract with you, our legitimate interests, your consent, and compliance with legal obligations. Our legitimate interests include operating, securing, improving, and supporting Staia, preventing fraud and abuse, understanding product usage, and enforcing our terms, balanced against your rights and interests.

You may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to processing, receive a portable copy of your data, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

If you apply for a role with us, we also process the personal information in your application (such as your contact details, CV, portfolio, interview and assessment notes, and reference information) to evaluate your application and manage our hiring process. Our legal bases are our legitimate interests in recruiting for our business and taking steps at your request before entering a contract, and your consent where we ask to keep your details for future roles. We retain unsuccessful applicants' data for 12 months and then delete it, unless you have consented to us keeping it longer. The rights, transfer safeguards, and complaint options described above apply equally to applicant data.

13. International Data Transfers

We and our service providers may process personal information in the United States, the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, and other countries where we or they operate. These countries may have data protection laws different from those in your country. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

14. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 17 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 17. If you believe a child under 17 has provided personal information to us, contact us at support@staia.io, and we will take appropriate steps to delete the information.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date above. If changes are material, we may provide additional notice, such as in-app notice or email where appropriate. Your continued use of the App after an updated policy becomes effective means you accept the updated policy, unless applicable law requires a different form of notice or consent.
